# Dr. Halima — Facebook Brand Guidelines

## Brand Feel
A caring, experienced doctor who explains things gently.  
Warm, maternal, and emotionally reassuring — while maintaining a premium specialist tone.  
Designed to feel safe for pregnant women and trustworthy for all audiences.

---

## 🎨 Brand Color Palette

| Color | HEX | Usage |
|------|-----|------|
| Soft Medical Pink | #EFA7B3 | Primary brand color, highlights |
| Warm Ivory | #FFF6F3 | Backgrounds, clean space |
| Deep Rose | #C65D7B | Call-to-action, headings |
| Sage Green | #A8C3A0 | Health tips, wellness |
| Soft Charcoal | #4A4A4A | Text color |

---

## 📌 Usage Guidelines
- Background → #FFF6F3  
- Titles → #C65D7B  
- Body Text → #4A4A4A  
- Highlights → #EFA7B3  
- Health Content → #A8C3A0  

---

## 🔘 CTA Usage
- Book Appointment → Deep Rose  
- Save Post → Soft Pink  
- Health Tip → Sage Green  

---

## ⚠️ Avoid
- Bright hot pink  
- Too many colors  
- Pure white + red hospital look
